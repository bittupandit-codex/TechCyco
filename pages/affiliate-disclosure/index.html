HOW PARTNER LINKS WORK

Affiliate Disclosure.

Some links on Techcyco may take you to third-party shopping websites. Here is what that can mean for you.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a tracked link to a partner or seller. If you follow one and make a qualifying purchase, Techcyco may receive a commission or referral benefit from that partner. Where applicable, this does not add a separate charge to your purchase.

Our recommendations

We aim to share products that may be useful to our visitors. A partner relationship does not change the price or terms set by the seller, and it should not replace your own review of the product, seller, customer ratings and return policy.

Third-party checkout

When you leave Techcyco to shop on a partner website, that website handles the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. The seller is responsible for confirming the order, payment, delivery and any return or refund.

Techcyco lists partner affiliations, including marketplaces shown on the website. Not every link is necessarily an affiliate link; qualifying links may provide a referral benefit.

For more information about an order, contact the seller shown at checkout or message Techcyco.
