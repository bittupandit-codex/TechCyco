Order support.

For help with a Techcyco order, send us a WhatsApp message with enough information to identify the order and understand the issue.

How to contact support

Message Techcyco on WhatsApp at +91 82717 34883.

Please include:

  • Your order reference or confirmation number.
  • The name or link of the product and the date you ordered it.
  • A short description of the issue and photos when relevant.

Orders placed with a marketplace

If you checked out on a partner or marketplace website, use that website’s order-help and return tools first. The marketplace or seller has the order status and controls fulfilment, delivery and refunds.

Keep your information safe

Do not send passwords, one-time passcodes (OTPs), full payment-card details or banking credentials. We will not need your password or OTP to look into an order.

Order support is available through the WhatsApp contact listed on this website. Response times may vary.
