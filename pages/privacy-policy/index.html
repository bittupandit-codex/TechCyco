Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what may happen to information you choose to share while using Techcyco’s website, contacting us or visiting a third-party shopping site.

Information you provide

If you start an order or contact us, you may choose to provide details such as your name, phone number, delivery address, order reference and the content of your message. The information requested will depend on the action you take.

How information is used

Information you send to Techcyco may be used to respond to questions, discuss or coordinate an order, provide customer support and address a reported issue. If you open WhatsApp, the information you choose to send is also handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

Third-party sellers and links

Techcyco may link to marketplaces, sellers or other services. If you follow a link, those third parties may collect information under their own privacy notices. Review their policies before sharing information or completing a purchase. Techcyco does not control their privacy practices.

Website forms and payment details

When an order form on this website opens a WhatsApp message, the form content is not sent until you choose to send that message in WhatsApp. Do not include passwords, OTPs or full payment credentials. Payments made on a third-party checkout are handled under that provider’s terms.

Choices and questions

You can choose what information to include when contacting us. To ask about information you have sent directly to Techcyco, use our contact page. Information held by WhatsApp or a marketplace must be managed through that provider.

Updates

We may update this notice when our website or services change. The version published on this page applies from the time it is posted.

This notice describes the website and contact flows currently presented by Techcyco. Third-party services have separate privacy policies.
