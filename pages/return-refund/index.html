RETURNS & REFUNDS

Let’s make it right.

If something is wrong with an item, contact the seller or our team as soon as possible. Return and refund eligibility depends on the product and where the order was placed.

Requesting help

  1. Contact the seller shown on your order confirmation, or message Techcyco for an order placed directly with us.
  2. Share your order reference, the issue and clear photos or video if the item is damaged, incorrect or incomplete.
  3. Wait for return instructions before sending an item back. Unrequested returns may not reach the correct seller or fulfilment address.

Eligibility and time limits

Return windows, eligible reasons, item condition requirements, exclusions and who pays return shipping are set out by the seller or marketplace for the specific product. Check those terms before ordering and contact the seller within the stated time limit if a problem occurs.

Refunds and replacements

Once a return or issue is reviewed, the seller will confirm whether a refund, replacement or another resolution is available. If approved, the seller or payment provider will communicate the method and processing time. Techcyco cannot promise a refund or replacement outside the applicable seller policy.

Orders through a partner

For purchases completed on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra or another partner website, submit the return or refund request through that seller’s order page and follow its current policy.

The product-specific checkout and seller policy take precedence for the order. Please confirm the return window and conditions before purchase.

For help with a direct order, visit Order Support or contact us.
