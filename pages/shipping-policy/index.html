DELIVERY INFORMATION

Shipping Policy.

Delivery options, charges and estimates can vary by product, seller and destination. Please check the details that apply to your specific order before confirming it.

Where we deliver

Available delivery locations are shown by the seller or checkout flow for the selected item. Some products may not be serviceable to every PIN code.

Delivery charges and estimates

Any applicable shipping fee and estimated delivery date should be shown or confirmed before you complete your purchase. Estimates are provided by the relevant seller or delivery partner and may change due to operational or weather conditions.

Order confirmation and tracking

An order is confirmed only after the relevant seller or Techcyco team confirms it. If tracking is available, it will be shared through the channel used for your purchase or by the seller.

Delays or delivery issues

If tracking has not updated, delivery is delayed, or your parcel appears lost or damaged, contact the seller shown on your order confirmation. For an order placed directly with Techcyco, message our Order Support with your order reference.

Third-party purchases

If you complete a purchase on a partner or marketplace website, that seller’s shipping options, estimates and terms apply to the order.

No fixed delivery timeline or shipping fee is promised on this page. Confirm the current estimate and charges at checkout before placing an order.
