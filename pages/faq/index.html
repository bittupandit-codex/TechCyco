Frequently asked questions.

A few helpful details about Techcyco products, orders and shopping partners.

What does Techcyco sell?

Techcyco features watches, belts, jewellery, sunglasses and other everyday finds. The range can change, so check the current product page or linked seller listing for availability and details.

How do I place an order?

Use the order instructions shown on the product page. Some orders can be started through WhatsApp; other products may link to an external shopping partner. Review the price, seller and order terms before confirming.

How can I check product availability or details?

Check the product page or partner listing for the most current information. You can also contact us before ordering.

When will my order arrive?

Delivery estimates and charges depend on the product, seller and delivery location. Check the estimate shown at checkout or ask us to confirm the details before you place an order. See our Shipping Policy.

Can I return an item or request a refund?

Eligibility depends on where and how the product was purchased and the applicable seller policy. Review the Return & Refund Policy and the terms shown at checkout. Contact us promptly if there is a problem.

How do I get help with an order?

Visit Order Support or message us on WhatsApp with your order reference. Do not send passwords, OTPs or payment credentials.

Does Techcyco use affiliate links?

Some product links may be affiliate links. Read our Affiliate Disclosure for details.
